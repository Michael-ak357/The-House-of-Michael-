Support Our Mission

Your support helps provide teachings, outreach, and resources for the community. We are grateful for those who feel called to contribute to the work of The House of Michael and The Spirit is God Fellowship.

Every donation, regardless of size, helps us continue our mission to prepare people for the second coming of the Messiah and lead them into the narrow path to salvation.

What Your Donations Support

Your generous contributions help us:

  • Produce Quality Sermons - Recording, editing, and publishing Bible teachings
  • Provide Resources - Study materials, guides, and educational content
  • Host Events - Bible studies, prayer meetings, and community gatherings
  • Reach More People - Online outreach and community engagement
  • Support Ministry Work - Operating costs for our fellowship
  • Help Those in Need - Assisting community members facing hardship
  • Expand Our Vision - Growing our ability to serve and teach

Biblical Foundation for Giving

"Each of you should give what you have decided in your heart to give, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver." - 2 Corinthians 9:7

"Honor the Lord with your wealth, with the firstfruits of all your crops; then your barns will be filled to overflowing, and your vats will brim with new wine." - Proverbs 3:9-10

"It is more blessed to give than to receive." - Acts 20:35

"For the love of money is a root of all kinds of evil. Some people, eager for money, have wandered from the faith and pierced themselves with many griefs. But you, man of God, flee from all this, and pursue righteousness, godliness, faith, love, endurance and gentleness." - 1 Timothy 6:10-11

"No one can serve two masters. Either you will hate the one and love the other, or you will be devoted to the one and despise the other. You cannot serve both God and money." - Matthew 6:24

"Jesus sat down opposite the place where the offerings were put and watched the crowd putting their money into the temple treasury. Many rich people threw in large amounts. But a poor widow came and put in two very small copper coins, worth only a few cents. Calling his disciples to him, Jesus said, 'Truly I tell you, this poor widow has put more into the treasury than all the others.'" - Mark 12:41-43
