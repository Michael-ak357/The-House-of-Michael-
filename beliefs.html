Core Beliefs

I seek out the truth by comparing scriptures with scriptures. For three or more scriptures will confirm one another. With this process, we can walk in the foundation of truth that God intended.

Summary of Michael's Beliefs

I believe that there is one true God, the Holy Spirit, who created the heavens, the earth, and all that exists. God is Spirit, and those who worship Him must worship Him in spirit and in truth (John 4:24). He alone is the Creator of all things (Genesis 1:1; Isaiah 45:18). I believe that Jesus Christ is the only begotten Son of God, conceived by the Holy Spirit and born of the virgin Mary (Matthew 1:18-23; Luke 1:35; John 3:16). He lived a perfect and sinless life, making Him the spotless Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world (John 1:29; 1 Peter 1:18-19). I believe that at His baptism the Holy Spirit descended upon Jesus and remained with Him. From that point forward, the Father, whom Jesus called God, dwelt within Him and worked through Him. As it is written: «"And Jesus, when he was baptized... he saw the Spirit of God descending like a dove, and lighting upon him." (Matthew 3:16) Jesus was then filled with the light of God.» «Jesus said; "The Father that dwelleth in me, he doeth the works." (John 14:10) In the conversation with Philip, Philip ask Jesus to show them the Father. The very question implies that they knew God was inside of Jesus somehow, but didn't know how.» «Jesus said; "The Son can do nothing of himself, but what he seeth the Father do." (John 5:19) This statement confirms that Jesus was following God.» For this reason, we believe that the authority, miracles, and words spoken during Jesus' ministry reveal the Father working through His Son. We believe that Jesus is the promised Messiah, the King of kings, the Lord's Anointed, and the Lamb of God. Through His death, burial, and resurrection He became the perfect sacrifice for the sins of the world, opening the way to eternal life for all who believe and obey God (Isaiah 53; John 3:16; Philippians 2:8-11). We believe that after Jesus ascended into heaven, the promised Holy Spirit came upon the disciples at Pentecost, just as Jesus had foretold (Acts 1:4-5; Acts 2:1-4). The Holy Spirit continues to dwell among humanity, convicting the world of sin, righteousness, and judgment (John 16:7-11), searching the hearts of men (Romans 8:27), and knowing those who truly belong to God (2 Timothy 2:19). We believe that God is presently gathering His harvest. The Holy Spirit is searching the hearts of men and women, calling them to repentance, holiness, and obedience. Jesus taught that "the harvest truly is plenteous" (Matthew 9:37-38), and the Scriptures declare that judgment begins with the house of God (1 Peter 4:17). We believe that when God has gathered His faithful people according to His perfect will, the Messiah will return in glory to receive His Kingdom (Matthew 24:30-31; 1 Thessalonians 4:16-17; Revelation 22:12). We believe that repentance is essential to salvation. Those who repent are forgiven, cleansed, and become new creatures in Christ. As it is written, "If any man be in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new" (2 Corinthians 5:17). Therefore, believers are called to be born again (John 3:3-8), to become as little children (Matthew 18:3), and to walk in holiness because God is holy (Leviticus 11:44-45; 1 Peter 1:15-16). We believe that eternal life belongs to those who endure in faith, obey God's commandments, and worship God in Spirit and in truth (Ecclesiastes 12:13; John 4:24; Revelation 14:12). Our desire is to seek the truth of Scripture, honor both the Father and His Son according to the Word of God, love one another, and prepare ourselves for the glorious return of Jesus Christ.

Our teachings focus on:

  • Faith - Trusting in God's word and promises
  • Repentance - Turning away from sin and toward God
  • Obedience to God - Following God's commandments and guidance
  • The Holy Spirit through Jesus Christ - The message and power of salvation

Scripture Comparison Method

We believe that God's truth is confirmed when multiple scriptures speak to the same principle or message. By comparing and analyzing scriptures side by side, we can:

  • Weed out unauthorized or misinterpreted teachings
  • Establish a strong foundation in biblical truth
  • Understand God's consistent message throughout Scripture
  • Discern false doctrines from true doctrine

This method ensures we are not relying on single interpretations, but on the confirmation of God's word itself.

Path to Salvation

Our mission is to prepare people for the second coming of the Messiah and to lead people into the narrow path to salvation. We believe salvation comes through:

  • Acceptance of Jesus Christ as Lord and Savior
  • Repentance from sin
  • Faith in God's grace and mercy
  • Obedience to God's commandments
  • The guidance and power of the Holy Spirit

Jesus said, "Enter through the narrow gate. For wide is the gate and broad is the road that leads to destruction, and many enter through it. But small is the gate and narrow the road that leads to life, and only a few find it." - Matthew 7:13-14

  • Prayer is powerful when done correctly and in the Spirit
  • Believing is the beginning of Faith, and knowing is Faith in practice

  • When the believers finally understand and walk in the truth of Gods law, Jesuswill return

Our desire is to create a safe space where believers can explore Scripture deeply, ask questions, and grow spiritually together.
