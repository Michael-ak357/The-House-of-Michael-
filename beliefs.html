Core Beliefs

We seek out the truth by comparing scriptures with scriptures. For three or more scriptures will confirm one another. With this process, we can walk in the foundation of truth that God intended.

Our teachings focus on:

  • Faith - Trusting in God's word and promises
  • Repentance - Turning away from sin and toward God
  • Obedience to God - Following God's commandments and guidance
  • The Holy Spirit through Jesus Christ - The message and power of salvation

Scripture Comparison Method

We believe that God's truth is confirmed when multiple scriptures speak to the same principle or message. By comparing and analyzing scriptures side by side, we can:

  • Weed out unauthorized or misinterpreted teachings
  • Establish a strong foundation in biblical truth
  • Understand God's consistent message throughout Scripture
  • Discern false doctrines from true doctrine

This method ensures we are not relying on single interpretations, but on the confirmation of God's word itself.

Path to Salvation

Our mission is to prepare people for the second coming of the Messiah and to lead people into the narrow path to salvation. We believe salvation comes through:

  • Acceptance of Jesus Christ as Lord and Savior
  • Repentance from sin
  • Faith in God's grace and mercy
  • Obedience to God's commandments
  • The guidance and power of the Holy Spirit

Jesus said, "Enter through the narrow gate. For wide is the gate and broad is the road that leads to destruction, and many enter through it. But small is the gate and narrow the road that leads to life, and only a few find it." - Matthew 7:13-14

Our Community

We are called to be a community that seeks truth together, supports one another in prayer, and grows together in faith. We believe:

  • Prayer is powerful when done in unity and in the Spirit
  • Fellowship strengthens our faith and understanding
  • We are called to correct misunderstandings with common sense and Scripture
  • God looks down on us as one people when we pray together in the Spirit

Our desire is to create a safe space where believers can explore Scripture deeply, ask questions, and grow spiritually together.

Our Mission

The House of Michael's assignment from God is to:

  • Correct misunderstandings using common sense and Scripture
  • Lead people into the narrow path to salvation
  • Prepare hearts for the second coming of the Messiah
  • Establish believers in the foundation of truth
  • Foster a community of prayer and spiritual growth
