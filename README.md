# The House of Michael - Christian Enlightenment Website

## Mission Statement
The House of Michael is dedicated to preparing people for the second coming of the Messiah through biblical truth and spiritual enlightenment. Our mission is to compare scriptures with scriptures, seeking the foundation of truth that God intended, and leading people into the narrow path to salvation.

## About Us
**The Spirit is God Fellowship** is a community dedicated to:
- Seeking truth through Scripture comparison
- Understanding God's purpose and message
- Growing in faith, repentance, and obedience
- Supporting one another through prayer and fellowship

We believe that by comparing three or more scriptures together, we can confirm God's truth and walk in the foundation He intended for us.
