The Vessel Chosen by God

Throughout Scripture, God reveals that He works through vessels chosen for His purpose. A vessel does not replace the one who fills it; it carries and reveals the power, will, and glory of the one who dwells within it. In the same way, Jesus Christ was chosen by God as His perfect vessel to bring salvation, truth, and reconciliation to mankind.

Jesus Speaks by God's Authority

Jesus Himself declared that He did not come by His own authority, but by the authority of the Father who sent Him:

"For I have not spoken of myself; but the Father which sent me, he gave me a commandment, what I should say, and what I should speak."
— John 12:49

Jesus continually pointed people toward God, showing that the power and works manifested through Him came from the Father:

"The Father that dwelleth in me, he doeth the works."
— John 14:10

Jesus Empowered by the Spirit

At His baptism, God came down and rested upon Jesus, empowering Jesus for His ministry:

"And the Holy Ghost descended in a bodily shape like a dove upon him, and a voice came from heaven, which said, Thou art my beloved Son; in thee I am well pleased."
— Luke 3:22

That voice actually came from the Spirit who descended upon Jesus. Jesus was then empowered by the Spirit to fulfill His purpose:

"Behold my servant, whom I uphold; mine elect, in whom my soul delighteth; I have put my spirit upon him."
— Isaiah 42:1

The Apostles' Testimony

The apostles also testified that God worked through Jesus:

"Jesus of Nazareth, a man approved of God among you by miracles and wonders and signs, which God did by him in the midst of you."
— Acts 2:22

God revealed Himself through Jesus: that through His Son, humanity could receive eternal life.

Jesus Glorifies the Father

Jesus is the vessel through whom God's love, power, and salvation were made known to the world. Jesus came not to glorify Himself, but to glorify the Father who sent Him:

"I have glorified thee on the earth: I have finished the work which thou gavest me to do."
— John 17:4

Conclusion

Jesus Christ is the chosen vessel of God—the Son through whom God revealed Himself. God demonstrated His power through Jesus, and offered salvation to mankind through His wisdom.

Through Jesus, we can see the heart of God, hear the words of God, and receive the promise of eternal life from God.

"For there is one God, and one mediator between God and men, the man Christ Jesus."
— 1 Timothy 2:5
