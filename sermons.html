Welcome to Our Sermon Series

Watch our Bible teachings and messages as we explore Scripture and seek greater understanding of God's purpose. Our sermons are designed to help you grow in faith, deepen your understanding of Scripture, and apply God's word to your daily life.

We believe that God's word is living and active, and through careful study and comparison of scriptures, we can uncover the truths that God has for us.

The Urgent Call to Walk in God's Law

The time is now. The call to obedience is urgent.

In these days, we must understand the critical importance of walking in the law of God. Since the Jewish people rejected Jesus as the Messiah, the kingdom was given to the Gentiles. Through the parables of Jesus and throughout Scripture, God reveals this truth: anyone who is obedient to the law of God will receive eternal life.

This is not optional. This is not a suggestion. This is the way to salvation, the way to eternal life, and the way to please God.

God's Law and Obedience

God's law is not a burden—it is the path to freedom, blessing, and eternal life. Throughout Scripture, we see the importance of obedience:

  • God's law is holy, righteous, and good - Romans 7:12
  • Obedience brings blessing and life - Deuteronomy 30:15-16
  • The law of God brings order and protection - Proverbs 29:18
  • Walking in God's commandments leads to eternal life - Matthew 19:16-17
  • Love is the fulfillment of the law - Romans 13:8-10

Jesus did not come to destroy the law, but to fulfill it and call us to a higher standard of obedience and righteousness.

The Parable of the Vineyard (Matthew 21:33-46)

Jesus taught a powerful parable about the rejection of the Messiah and what it means for obedience and salvation:

"Hear another parable: There was a landowner who planted a vineyard. He put a wall around it, dug a winepress in it and built a tower. Then he rented the vineyard to some farmers and moved to another place. When the harvest time approached, he sent his servants to the tenants to collect his fruit. But the tenants seized his servants; they beat one, killed another, and stoned a third. Then he sent other servants to them, more than the first time, and the tenants treated them the same way. Last of all, he sent his son, thinking, 'They will respect my son.' But when the tenants saw the son, they said to each other, 'This is the heir. Come, let's kill him and take his inheritance.' So they took him and threw him out of the vineyard and killed him. Therefore, when the owner of the vineyard comes, what will he do to those tenants? He will bring those wretches to a wretched end, and he will rent the vineyard to other tenants, who will give him his fruit at harvest time." - Matthew 21:33-41

The Message: The vineyard represents God's kingdom. The Jewish leaders rejected Jesus, the Son of God. As a result, the kingdom was taken from them and given to others—the Gentiles—who will be obedient and faithful to God. Those who are obedient to God's law will inherit the kingdom and receive eternal life.

The Parable of the Talents (Matthew 25:14-30)

Jesus teaches about the importance of obedience and faithfulness:

"It will be like a man going on a journey, who called his servants and entrusted his wealth to them. To one he gave five bags of gold, to another two bags, and to another one bag, each according to his ability. Then he went on his journey. The man who had received five bags of gold went at once and put his money to work and gained five more... His master replied, 'Well done, good and faithful servant! You have been faithful with a few things; I will put you in charge of many things. Come and share your master's happiness!'" - Matthew 25:14-21

The Message: God has given us His law and His commandments. Those who are obedient and faithful in following God's law will be rewarded with eternal life and glory. The unfaithful servant who did not obey will be cast out into darkness.

The Parable of the Sheep and Goats (Matthew 25:31-46)

Jesus reveals the eternal consequence of obedience and disobedience:

"When the Son of Man comes in his glory, and all the angels with him, he will sit on his glorious throne. All the nations will be gathered before him, and he will separate the people one from another as a shepherd separates the sheep from the goats... Then the King will say to those on his right, 'Come, you who are blessed by my Father; take your inheritance, the kingdom prepared for you since the creation of the world... whatever you did for the least of these brothers and sisters of mine, you did for me.' Then he will say to those on his left, 'Depart from me, you who are cursed, into the eternal fire prepared for the devil and his angels.'" - Matthew 25:31-41

The Message: Obedience to God's law means caring for others, showing compassion, and walking in righteousness. Those who obey will enter the kingdom and receive eternal life. Those who disobey will be cast out.

Eternal Life Through Obedience

Scripture is clear about the path to eternal life:

"Now we know that God's judgment against those who do such things is based on truth. So when you, a mere human, pass judgment on them and yet do the same things, do you think you will escape God's judgment? Or do you show contempt for the riches of his kindness, forbearance and patience, not realizing that God's kindness is intended to lead you to repentance?" - Romans 2:2-4

"If you love me, keep my commands... Whoever has my commands and keeps them is the one who loves me. The one who loves me will be loved by my Father, and I too will love them and show myself to them." - John 14:15, 21

"Now I saw a new heaven and a new earth, for the first heaven and the first earth had passed away. And I John saw the holy city, New Jerusalem, coming down out of heaven from God, prepared as a bride adorned for her husband. And I heard a great voice out of heaven saying, Behold, the tabernacle of God is with men, and he will dwell with them, and they shall be his people, and God himself shall be with them, and be their God. And God shall wipe away all tears from their eyes; and there shall be no more death, neither sorrow, nor crying, neither shall there be any more pain: for the former things are passed away." - Revelation 21:1-4

Those who walk in obedience to God's law will inherit this eternal kingdom and experience perfect peace, joy, and the presence of God forever.

The Call to Action

The time is now. The choice is yours. Will you walk in the law of God and receive eternal life? Or will you turn away and face separation from God?

"I have set before you life and death, blessings and curses. Now choose life, so that you and your children may live and that you may love the Lord your God, listen to his voice, and hold fast to him." - Deuteronomy 30:15-16, 19-20

Walking in God's law is not burdensome—it is the path to true freedom, purpose, and eternal life. The question is: Will you answer the call?
